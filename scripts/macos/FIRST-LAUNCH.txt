Klipparkiv for macOS
====================

The app is not notarized by Apple. macOS will ask for confirmation on the
first launch. This is expected and only happens once.

1. Drag Klipparkiv into Applications.
2. Open Klipparkiv.
3. If macOS blocks it:
   - macOS 15 and newer: System Settings -> Privacy & Security ->
     scroll down -> Open Anyway.
   - macOS 14 and older: right-click Klipparkiv -> Open -> Open.
4. Enter your login password if asked.

Terminal alternative:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Klipparkiv.app

Data location:
  Library and media: ~/Movies/Klipparkiv
  Settings, updates, backups and logs: ~/Library/Application Support/Klipparkiv
  WKWebView may keep its own cache under ~/Library.

License and notices:
  see LICENSE.txt, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, PRIVACY.md, SECURITY.md and LICENSES/
  in this DMG. The app bundle also carries a copy under Contents/Resources/licenses.

Use Klipparkiv only for media you own or are allowed to save. Updates are
installed inside the app and do not require this confirmation again. If
automatic replacement is not possible, the app downloads the DMG and asks you
to drag it into Applications.
